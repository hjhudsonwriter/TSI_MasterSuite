# Artwork, sounds and videos

All art, sound and video in this suite comes from Harry Hudson's own Scarlett Isles tool repos (listed in `docs/ASSETS.md`). It's for Harry's private table.

Several pictures carry embedded Content Credentials (C2PA) saying they were made with AI image tools (ChatGPT / GPT-4o, or OpenAI's Sora), for example the hub logo, `hero.png`, the wide crest logo and the Notice Board's board art. `hero.png` includes an AI-drawn dragon-and-ampersand that resembles the D&D trademark. That's fine at a private table, but worth reviewing if the suite is ever shared publicly.
