1. Training grades and progression
A regiment begins as Regular with the unchanged base archetype profile. Its first completed drill grants Trained; a second, longer drill grants Veteran. The steps are sequential. Specialist doctrine is a separately purchased and individually applied R&D training qualification, not an automatic reward given to every regiment of the same archetype.
Regular: standard recruitment, unchanged original War Table statistics.
Trained: first drill programme; modest permanent resilience improvement.
Veteran: second drill programme; keeps its Trained bonuses and adds Cohesion.
Specialist doctrine: later R&D programme (Infantry / Archery / Cavalry / Command & Logistics); at most one per regiment in the initial design.
Both ordinary grades become available at War Room Level 2; Level 4 adds the separate R&D specialist-training route.

One physical regiment can take only one drill at a time; multiple regiments of the same type may train concurrently in separate slots.

A new training grade applies only after its programme is fully completed.
5. Recouping: the campaign owner’s already specified rule
Any surviving formation that has lost soldiers gains a Recouping tag and automatically regains personnel gradually. The recovery period is based on missing soldiers as a share of that archetype’s full regiment size. Training is unavailable until headcount is completely restored.

Example: Line Infantry at 41 / 100 has lost 59%; it receives the Recouping tag for 10 days. Soldiers return progressively, with any fractional remainder resolved by the final day. At full strength the tag disappears and the unit becomes drill-eligible.
Existing completed training grades and specialist doctrines survive casualties and replenishment.
Understrength battlefield Cohesion and Battle Value continue to scale using the existing personnel-based rules.
Depleted units remain deployable under existing war rules; Recouping pauses while they are away, then resumes or is recalculated based on their updated remaining strength.
Existing War Room Recruit replenishment must update the same regiment and not create a fresh, untrained identity; a completed recruitment replacement clears Recouping without duplicating personnel.
If all soldiers are lost, retain the existing destroyed-regiment outcome.
Boundary clarification proposed: exactly 20% goes into 3 days, 40% into 7 and 60% into 10; this resolves the minor gaps in the shorthand “<20 / 21–40 / 41–60 / >60”.
6. War Room Level 3: Regimental Training Grounds
Level 3 unlocks two concurrent drills and a new Coordinated Muster order. Coordinated Muster lasts seven days and recruits or replenishes two eligible formations, in any supported combination (for example, Archers and Heavy Infantry, or Line Infantry and one Lieutenant). Base sizes, base stats and the original upkeep rules remain unchanged.
An accelerated two-formation order is a higher administrative capacity, not free training, a special combat modifier or an automatic new unit type. The existing upfront recruitment cost stays unchanged.
7. War Room Level 5: Grand War College
Level 5 unlocks a third concurrent drill slot and Grand Muster, which recruits or replenishes three eligible formations in one seven-day order under existing recruitment and upkeep costs.

9. Implementation and save-data safeguards
The existing Military list may group identical full-strength regiments into a row with a quantity, while depleted regiments receive their own row and identifier. Training requires stable per-regiment identity underneath any grouped display; separate Line Infantry formations might be Regular, Trained and Veteran simultaneously.
Create durable unique IDs for every physical regiment and migrate legacy grouped entries without losing the number of formations.
Keep base archetype definitions in war-units-data.js unchanged; apply overlay modifiers only when building each individual player regiment’s effective War Table profile.
Preserve grades, training progress, doctrine choice and original identity through save/load, campaign imports, deployment, battle results and resupply.
Ensure an ongoing training order exclusively reserves its chosen individual regiment; never train a duplicate represented by the same grouped quantity.
Prevent double spending of gold, duplicated soldiers, repeated training awards and repeated Morale-charge consumption through persistent receipt/processing rules.
Integrate automatic Recouping with existing recruitment-first replenishment and war absence mechanics.
Keep baseline lieutenant leadership, beast stats, War AI, mission-generated opposition and casualty rules unchanged.
Add regression tests for legacy saves, multiple same-type regiments with different grades, battles during training, pauses, zero casualties, partial casualties and unit destruction.
