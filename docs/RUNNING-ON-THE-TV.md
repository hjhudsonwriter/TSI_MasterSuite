# Running the Tidal Sequence on the TV (plain English)

1. Open the suite on your laptop and go to **Pelagosi Puzzle Trials**, then choose **The Tidal Sequence**.
2. In the right-hand column, under Trial Notes, press **Open Player View**. A second, plain window opens. The little label next to the button says **Connected** once the two windows are talking.
3. Drag that second window onto the TV. **Double-click it** to make it fill the screen. Double-click again to go back.
4. Run the puzzle from your laptop as normal. The TV window shows only the chamber, the pressure word (Calm to Surge), the wall and the title of what has just happened. Your readings, buttons, hints and rules never appear on it.
5. **Sound comes from the laptop.** The TV window is silent. If the sound should come from the TV, set the laptop's sound output to the TV (HDMI).
6. If you refresh the TV window, or reload the suite on your laptop, the two find each other again within about two seconds. No clicking needed.
7. The mouse pointer hides itself on the TV window after two seconds.
