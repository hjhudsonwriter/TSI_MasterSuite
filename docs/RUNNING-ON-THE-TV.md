# Running the Tidal Sequence on the TV (plain English)

1. Open the suite on your laptop and go to **Pelagosi Puzzle Trials**, then choose **The Tidal Sequence**.
2. In the right-hand column, under Trial Notes, press **Open Player View**. A second, plain window opens. The little label next to the button says **Connected** once the two windows are talking.
3. Drag that second window onto the TV. **Double-click it** to make it fill the screen. Double-click again to go back.
4. Run the puzzle from your laptop as normal. The TV window shows only the chamber, the pressure word (Calm to Surge), the wall and the title of what has just happened. Your readings, buttons, hints and rules never appear on it.
5. **Sound.** Sound effects and voice lines come from the laptop, as before (set the laptop's sound output to the TV if you want them there). Since 5 November 2026 the Explorer's **background sound** (the road sound, weather, camp, towns, fights and moods) comes from the **Explorer Player View on the TV**, so it carries on when you change tool on the laptop. If that window is the TV's screen, its sound comes out of the TV. The first time, the browser may ask for one click in the TV window ("Click here once to turn the sound on"). If no Player View is open, the background sound plays from the laptop's Explorer window instead, stops when you change tool, and a small note says so. The Soundscape button in the top bar sets the volume, mute and mood. The Tidal Sequence's own sound still comes from the laptop.
6. If you refresh the TV window, or reload the suite on your laptop, the two find each other again within about two seconds. No clicking needed.
7. The mouse pointer hides itself on the TV window after two seconds.
8. **Cinematic videos.** START SEQUENCE, the Anchor coming out and the Absence each play a short silent film on the TV window. Your laptop screen shows "Cinematic playing" with a Skip button (Esc also skips). If no TV window is open, the film plays on your laptop instead so you can test it on one screen. Untick **Play cinematic videos** in the Readings panel to turn them all off. They play silently: the sound is always from the laptop.
9. **Your cursor on the TV.** While your pointer is over the map (Explorer), the painting (Bastion) or the chamber (Tidal Sequence), the TV shows a small gold arrow in the same place, and a ring when you click. Untick **Show my cursor to players** (next to each Open Player View button) to turn it off.
