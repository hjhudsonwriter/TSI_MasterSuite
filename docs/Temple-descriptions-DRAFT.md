# Draft descriptions for the three First Temples

**Status: a draft for Harry's review. Nothing here is in the suite yet.** Each text would sit under the temple's painting in the Explorer's place pop-up (the DM's window, and the players' window on the TV), in the `description` line of the temple's pin in `tools/explorer/data/explorer-data.js`.

**What I used, and what I did not.** I wrote only from your brief (Telluria: roots at the base of two mountains, Midland Province; Aurush: a mountain peak at dawn, Western Province (South); Pelagos: a frozen, icy coastline, The North Isle) and from what the three paintings actually show. I have **not** invented any history, priests, names, rites or secrets: nothing about who built them, how old they are, or what is inside. Because players read these on the TV, there is nothing here a DM would want kept back. If you would like lore in them, tell me what is true and I will add it.

Spellings follow the suite: Telluria, Aurush, Pelagos, Midland Province, Western Province (South), The North Isle.

---

## First Temple of Telluria — Midland Province

**Full:** Where the Midland Province climbs into stone, the First Temple of Telluria stands at the foot of two great mountains. Roots as thick as old oaks wind over its carved pillars and frame the great doors, and pines grow from the stonework itself. Lanterns burn along the steps, and robed worshippers climb slowly toward the glow within.

**Short (one line):** At the foot of two great mountains, a temple of carved stone bound in roots.

## First Temple of Aurush — Western Province (South)

**Full:** High above a sea of cloud, the First Temple of Aurush crowns a mountain peak, lit by the first light of dawn. A stair of pale stone climbs through the crags, with a brazier at every turn and crimson sun-banners hanging from its walls. At the summit, fires burn on the temple's towers, and the whole province lies spread out below.

**Short (one line):** A sun-banner temple on a mountain peak, above the clouds at dawn.

## First Temple of Pelagos — The North Isle

**Full:** On the frozen coast of the North Isle, the First Temple of Pelagos rises from black rock and breaking surf, its stone hung with ice. Two hooded statues keep watch on either side of the doors, and pale blue tide-patterns glow in the carved walls. The sea crashes against the barred arches beneath the steps, and the storm never seems to leave.

**Short (one line):** A temple of ice-hung stone and glowing blue carving, battered by the northern sea.

2. **Anything true I should add?** For example what each temple is known for, who tends it, or what the party can do there. I have left all of that out on purpose.
