# Draft descriptions for the 11 new Workshop items

**Status: a draft for Harry's review. Nothing here is in the suite yet.** Every line is taken from, or built directly on, the Beast-Catcher's Guide (6 November 2026) and Harry's capture rulings (10 November 2026). Change anything you like; when you are happy, say so and I will put it into the Bastion's Compendium (the "summary" line shown under each item's name).

How each entry is laid out: **Type** (the small label), **Summary** (the description the Compendium would show), and a **Rules note** (what it does in a capture, so the text and the rules cannot drift apart). The Rules note is for you; the Summary is what players and the DM would read.

The rule behind all of them: each beast has three best-fit items, and each best-fit item the party carries lowers that beast's Settle DC by 2. An item that does not suit the beast does nothing for it. "Used up" items are spent when the creature is granted; the others stay in the Travel bags.

---

## 1. Beast-Catcher's Kit
- **Crafted by:** Workshop · Handyman
- **Type:** Beast-Catcher's gear (used up)
- **Summary:** A weighted capture net, binding ropes and a handler's whip in one roll. The net restrains a Large or smaller beast (DC 10 Strength to escape), and the ropes and whip handle the tie-down and control. No party can attempt a capture without one.
- **Rules note:** Required. No kit in the Travel bags, no grant. One kit is used up on each attempt. Suits all six beasts but does not itself lower the DC.

## 2. Beast-Collar & Lead
- **Crafted by:** Workshop · Smith
- **Type:** Beast-Catcher's gear (reusable)
- **Summary:** A fitted collar and lead for a calm beast. It makes handling the creature, and leading it home, much easier.
- **Rules note:** Best fit for Fellcat, Dire Wolf and Griffin: −2 to their Settle DC each. Reusable.

## 3. Beast-Chain Rig
- **Crafted by:** Workshop · Smith
- **Type:** Beast-Catcher's gear (reusable)
- **Summary:** A forge-chain harness for the strongest beasts. If the beast wakes early, it needs a DC 20 Strength check to break free.
- **Rules note:** Best fit for Buckbear, Sabrecat and Wyvern: −2 each. Reusable. Also covers the carry home for these.

## 4. Stillroot Salve
- **Crafted by:** Workshop · Alchemist
- **Type:** Beast-Catcher's salve (used up)
- **Summary:** A Tellurian calming salve. It lowers a beast's aggression without sedating it.
- **Rules note:** Best fit for Fellcat, Griffin and Buckbear: −2 each. Used up when the creature is granted.

## 5. Dreamroot Salve
- **Crafted by:** Workshop · Alchemist
- **Type:** Beast-Catcher's salve (used up)
- **Summary:** A sedative salve that works only on a beast at or below half its hit points. A failed dose only poisons the beast, and an overdose can kill a smaller one.
- **Rules note:** Best fit for Buckbear, Sabrecat and Wyvern: −2 each. Used up when the creature is granted.

## 6. Beast Salve
- **Crafted by:** Workshop · Alchemist
- **Type:** Beast-Catcher's salve (used up)
- **Summary:** Stabilises and heals the captive on the way home, so that a hard fight does not cost the party the beast.
- **Rules note:** Suits every beast, but it heals the captive and does **not** lower the Settle DC. Used up when the creature is granted.

## 7. Scent Lure
- **Crafted by:** Workshop · Alchemist
- **Type:** Beast-Catcher's lure (used up)
- **Summary:** A strong-smelling bait that draws the beast onto ground the party chooses, giving advantage on the approach or the ambush.
- **Rules note:** Best fit for Fellcat, Dire Wolf and Griffin: −2 each. Used up when the creature is granted.

## 8. Falconer's Hood & Jesses
- **Crafted by:** Workshop · Artisan
- **Type:** Beast-Catcher's gear (reusable)
- **Summary:** A hood and leather straps that calm a raptor-type beast and keep its head and talons under control.
- **Rules note:** Best fit for the Griffin only: −2. Reusable.

## 9. Jaw-Strap & Tail Sheath
- **Crafted by:** Workshop · Artisan
- **Type:** Beast-Catcher's gear (reusable)
- **Summary:** Binds the jaws and caps the stinger. The only safe way to handle a wyvern.
- **Rules note:** Best fit for the Wyvern only: −2. Reusable.

## 10. Handler's Gauntlets
- **Crafted by:** Workshop · Artisan
- **Type:** Beast-Catcher's gear (reusable)
- **Summary:** Heavy forearm guards for grappling a beast and fitting its gear. They give advantage on the grapple and the tie-down.
- **Rules note:** Best fit for Buckbear and Sabrecat: −2 each. Reusable.

## 11. Beast Muzzle
- **Crafted by:** Workshop · Artisan
- **Type:** Beast-Catcher's gear (reusable)
- **Summary:** Stops the bite while the party works.
- **Rules note:** Best fit for Fellcat, Dire Wolf and Sabrecat: −2 each. Reusable.

## Questions for you

2. **How much rules text in the Summary?** The drafts keep to the guide's own wording and leave "−2 to the Settle DC" in the Rules note only, so players read flavour and the DM reads the numbers. Say if you would rather the Summary carried the DC effect and which beasts each suits (the Beast-Catcher's Guide tab already shows that).
3. **Cost and weight.** Other filled-in items show a cost and weight ("Weight: 5 lb • Cost: 2 gp"). The guide gives neither for these. I have left them out; give me figures if you want them.
