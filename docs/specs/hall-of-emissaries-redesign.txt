1. EXECUTIVE DECISION
The existing Level 4 “Negotiate Preferred Supplier Contract” and Level 5 “Negotiate Major 
Commercial Accord” proposals are WITHDRAWN; do not code them.
A separate Hall redesign will be commissioned in a future phase.
Until then preserve existing working Hall orders and legacy data, all original Port 
Charter/commodity/shipping functionality, and upgradeability to L5 without new orders.
No new Commercial Treaties panel.
2. WHY THIS DIRECTION
The Scarlett Isles suite serves D&D exploration, roleplay and campaign story, not a second 
standalone commerce management game.
The new physical commodity Port Charter network, earned by visiting ports and negotiating 
with NPC officials, is preferred over generic roll-and-generate-income trade orders.
Economic development facilities make real goods, Warehouse stocks them, Dock physically 
ships and sells them.
Diplomatic conduct shapes permission, opportunity, sanctions, standing, and future quests.
CORE PRINCIPLE
THE ADVENTURE CREATES THE OPPORTUNITY; THE BASTION MAKES IT 
CONSEQUENTIAL.

5. APPROVED TRADE CONSORTIUM DIRECTION
KEEP the consortium’s narrative and political concept, and move its eventual functionality 
from abstract income to a negotiated physical-goods partnership fulﬁlled using existing Port 
Charter, Warehouse and Dock.
A Consortium must identify:
Required Consortium detail
Clan and chartered destination
Speciﬁc commodity IDs
Quantities/lots
Number or frequency of shipments
Fulﬁlment deadline
Negotiated prices/beneﬁts
Penalties
Relationship impacts
Successful payments occur only on actual delivered/sold cargo using the existing shipping 
engine, not passive synthetic gold.
A missed shipment may create a new diplomatic dilemma, grace period or negotiation 
request instead of instantly punishing random maritime misfortune.
ILLUSTRATIVE DRAFT MATERIAL — NOT APPROVED
Risks, rewards, the sample 14-day schedule, prices and thresholds are examples only, 
NOT approved.
Consortium is likely a ﬂagship Hall L5 capability, not an extra duplicate trade engine or panel. 
This is PROPOSED / SUBJECT TO DESIGN, not conﬁrmed.
6. APPROVED HYBRID DIPLOMATIC EVENT SYSTEM
Two supported modes are speciﬁed:
Mode Supported direction
(a) DM-AUTHORED Events based on campaign story, with the 
DM editing participants, situation, 
choice/outcome and tracked consequences.
(b) Optional PROCEDURALLY OFFERED Event templates with scene, clan(s), 2–4 
decision choices, speciﬁc possible results 
and optional checks.
The user can approve, edit, defer or dismiss an optional procedurally offered event BEFORE 
the event affects canon/state.
Never autonomously change major political facts.
The event library of approximately 20 separately drafted prototypes will be reviewed by the 
user and provided LATER; do not invent event lore or make these events canon now.
Existing 28-day Bastion event cadence is a proposed low-interruption integration, NOT an 
approved schedule; ideally let the DM adjust it, and do not add a second spammy popup 
system.
