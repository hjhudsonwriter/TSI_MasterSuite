# S11 built: the Temple

Step S11 of the Strip Back Overhaul (`docs/STRIP-BACK-PLAN.md`, Harry's decision 2; design in `docs/strip-back/F-temple-hall-dock.md` sections 0 to 2 and 6 to 10, `A-facilities.md`, `B-panels-war.md` 2.1 and 4, `J-cross-cutting.md` 4.3 and 8.1). Branch `temple`. The three Shrines are now ONE Temple, and the Favour of the Gods panel is gone (its bars are in the Temple). Nothing was pushed.

## Harry's decisions as built

- **A new facility `temple`, named "Temple"**: party level 8, a construction slot, 21 days, **one level only** (`singleLevel`, so no Upgrade row). The world text "Temple of ..." in `shared/data/regions.js` is untouched.
- **The panel is split into thirds, side by side**: Telluria, Aurush, Pelagos. Each has the god's name, the god's Favour bar (0 to 100%; the saved `favour` map is unchanged), **Pray to (god)**, a result line, and **Receive Blessing** (a dropdown of exactly three blessings and a button, both greyed out and not selectable until that god's bar is full, with a line saying how full it is). Each third also shows that god's painting, its Great Rites (waiting at the Ironbow or with the party) and, for Pelagos, the Blessings held for the Dock.
- **Pray to (god) is instant**: the Bastion rolls a d50 with its own `rand`, adds a whole number 1 to 50 to the bar (the bar stops at 100) and says the roll in the result line and the Day Log ("Rolled a d50: 26. Aurush's bar goes from 1% to 27% (+26%)."). It replaces the 1-day Pray order and its 1d20.
- **Receive Blessing is instant and spends the WHOLE bar** (back to 0%). It makes no order. Blessings: Pelagos: Voyage Blessing, Bless Commercial Shipment, Great Rite of Pelagos. Aurush: Blessed Lantern, Consecrate Expedition Supplies, Great Rite of Aurush. Telluria: Earthward Talisman, Rootguard Salve, Great Rite of Telluria. What each does is what it did: goods into the Warehouse (1 talisman, 2 doses of salve, 1 lantern, 3 Cleansing Portions, 1 Voyage Blessing), one Blessing of Pelagos held for the Dock (-10 points of voyage risk, read when a ship sails), or a Great Rite that waits at the Ironbow until the DM gives it to the party on an expedition in the Explorer.
- **Dropped**: Craft and its six charms, the hint and long-rest prayers, Purification Incense, the Aquatic Protection Charm and Bless Bastion Foundations. Their goods (and the Courier's and the Travel bags' names for them) stay, so anything already made keeps its name.
- **The Favour of the Gods panel, its bottom-bar button and Claim are removed.** The Favour numbers still feed the DM doc ("Where the party is" shows the god card) and Change A Score for Favour (0 to 100) works as before (tested).
- **Texts**: party items "(from the Temple)", the Explorer's message "Temple blessing", the Courier's "and the Temple's items", the bridge refusal "a Great Rite the Temple doesn't make", the Dock's protection row `facId: 'temple'`.
- **Map and tile**: the Temple's map overlay draws all three Shrine buildings (three `map.overlays` rows for `temple`), labelled at each (`mapLabels.temple` has `also` for the other two points); its tile shows the three Shrine paintings side by side as strips (`facilityImages.temple` is a list); each third of the panel shows its own god's painting. No picture file was moved or deleted.

## The conversion: step `shrinesToTemple`

A registry step above `sweepFacilities` (pure, idempotent, audit-clean, no dice). It works on any save with a Shrine slot, level, repair or order, so a version 4 save that an older copy of the suite overwrote is converted again with the "tidied again" notice.

- **Slots**: the Temple takes the slot of a built Shrine (the first, if several are built), else the one that would be ready first (kept "being built"); the others are free; `facilityLevels.temple` is 1; the three old ids go from levels, repairs and research and are recorded in `archivedFacilities` with their names. A save that already has a Temple keeps it. No Temple is ever built for free (unlike the Beast Pen: nothing is lost by its absence, since Favour, Great Rites and held Blessings belong to the Bastion).
- **Favour, Great Rites, a held Blessing of Pelagos**: untouched (the audit's I6 checks Great Rites and the held blessing come back identical).
- **Orders that carry on (MOVE)**: the nine blessings the Temple still gives (the Temple has a blessing function of that id and that god). They keep id, due day, `issuedDay`, `costGP`, `favourCost`, `inputs`, `chosen`; only `facId` and `label` change ("Temple: Rootguard Salve"). They complete on their due days through `completeSimpleOrder` because the Temple's functions have the old ids. The Favour they took is not given back (they are paid for).
- **Orders that end (CANCEL, with everything given back)**: Craft, every Pray (the old Favour prayer too: the new Pray is instant and its roll is simply not made; it cost nothing), Purification Incense, the Aquatic Protection Charm, Bless Bastion Foundations and any Upgrade. A cancelled order's Favour goes back to its god's bar (cap 100, the overflow said), its goods to the Warehouse, its gold to the treasury. Each is listed with a plain reason.
- **The words** (built in the step): "The Shrine of Telluria, the Shrine of Aurush and the Shrine of Pelagos have become one Temple. Each god has a Favour bar there, a Pray button (instant: it adds a d50 roll, 1 to 50 percent, to the bar) and Receive Blessing... Building work on the Shrine of Aurush stopped (building costs no gold). Your Favour bars carry over as they are, and what you already hold is kept: one Great Rite (Cleansing Radiance) and one Blessing of Pelagos for the Dock." Then the Favour, Goods and Orders paragraphs the shared helpers write.
- **Forecast**: for a version 3 save read before it is converted, `TSI.campaign.cancelledByConversion` agrees with the step for every old Shrine function (a test runs every one through the real conversion).
