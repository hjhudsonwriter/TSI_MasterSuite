## What Harry sees

- **The Dock** has four tabs: **Cargo** (it opens on this one: the main button Ship cargo… is in view at the top, then the Charters you can ship to, the voyage protection, ships at sea, standing shipments, recent sales), **Orders** (Upgrade, Charter Berth and **Register Port Charter**, a 3-day order, no gold, no roll), **Port Charters** (each Charter with its state, terms, the estimate, Amend terms…, Reset to standard terms… and the history: the Hall's box moved over whole) and **Routes** (the map of the Scarlett Isles).
- **The Routes map** glows for each Clan that has a **registered** Port Charter (one lane for the ports of one Clan: ten ports share seven lanes) and glows brighter, and a little faster, while a ship is at sea to that Clan. A Charter that is only earned, or being registered, lights nothing but is listed beside the map with where it stands and any ship on the way. With "reduce motion" on, the pulse stops and the glow is steady.
- **The Hall of Emissaries** has one level and no orders. Its panel shows the seven Clans' Honour/Respect and Political Capital to read only (it says they change only through Change a score in the DM doc, or a confirmed Diplomatic Event), **Diplomatic Events** (the old Diplomatic Records ledger, with its **Record an event…** button, unchanged inside), a read-only **Earlier agreements and routes** list when there is any history, and Demolish… at the foot. No Upgrade, Routes, Resolve, Council Ledger, Trade Network or Clear Diplomacy Records. No diplomatic incident happens by itself.
- **An old Bastion** is converted on its first open with a plain-English paragraph in "The Bastion has changed": the Register order moved, the Hall's waiting orders cancelled with their gold back (each by name), the running contracts and routes paid out once and ended, delegations/summits/Favour Tokens/disputes ended, and what the Hall is now.

## Decisions I made myself

1. **Four tabs, not three:** Cargo, Orders, Port Charters, Routes, opening on Cargo, so the one crimson main button (Ship cargo…) is in view as the panel opens (F 5.4). Orders is its own tab so the panel stays short; `reveal` and the tests switch tab for you.
2. **The Routes map is inline in the Dock's Routes tab**, not a pop-up, with the map left and the Charter/ship list right (the picture is portrait, 1494 x 1996).
3. **Glow rules:** registered Charter = steady lane; ship at sea (outbound or returning, Clan from its Charter first, else the ship's own record) = brighter. Only Clan-level art exists, so there are no port pins and no ship icons (F 5.3 says new art would be needed).
4. **`reduce motion` stops the pulse** (the rest of the suite slows its signature animations instead); a steady glow is shown.
5. **Final settlement counts a route only while its Consortium lives** (a route with no matching Consortium would have expired at the next day's tick) and stops at the day before the Consortium's last day. High-risk routes pay the expected "success" amount (no dice).
6. **Favour Tokens end** (set to 0); `diplomacy` stays as `{ tokens }` because the Clan Influence panel (S10) reads it. S10 can delete `diplomacy` and the pill.
7. **`legacyDiplomacy` is a flat list of records** (contracts, routes, delegations and Summits), shown collapsed under the Hall's Diplomatic Events; it is shifted by Reset Travel.
8. **Quiet cleanup:** empty old fields (`arbitration`, the old `diplomacy` lists, default-valued trade fields) are dropped without a report line; only real leftovers make a line, so a plain Bastion still gets "nothing of yours changed".
9. **The ledger is called Diplomatic Events** in the panel and its dialogs ("Record a diplomatic event" was already the editor's title); the code and the saved field keep the old name `diplomaticRecords`.
10. **`R.finishRoutes` keeps its name** and the day's `trade` stage is unchanged (Dock voyages and parcels settle there); renaming would have touched many tests for nothing.
11. **The dice-box machinery** (`rollD20`, Resolve, `skipOrder`) stays in the code because the War Council still uses it; once the war lane merges nothing asks for a typed d20 inside a day, and it can be removed in the clean-up step. The four phase9 checks about it were Hall-driven and were deleted (listed in the known-issues draft).
