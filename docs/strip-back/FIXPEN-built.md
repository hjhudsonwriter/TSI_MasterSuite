# FIXPEN built: the independent review's fixes to the Beast Pen (S14) and the Beast Handler's Bond page (S15)

Branch `fixpen`, worktree `wt-fixpen`, made from `main` (22 October 2026). Nothing pushed. The review (six lenses, two sceptics each) found the defects below; all thirteen are fixed and tested. Entries for the lead to renumber into `docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md` are at the end ("Known-issues draft").

## What was fixed, in the order of the fix list

1. **Elara's wyvern is no longer lost.** Her House order moves to the Pen without a record in the Bastion's list of creatures (she is recorded only when the order completes). When the Pen already held a wyvern, completing the order used to say "nothing changed" and the order ended, so she was gone and the Explorer cannot grant her again. Now `R.completeHousing` records her as a **waiting** beast (with the relationship the story gave her: Bond 1, handler Elara) and says so in the log, exactly like a granted beast waiting for a home. The same safety is applied when the DM **cancels** her order (`R.cancelOrder`) or **demolishes** the Pen while only her order is pending (`R.demolishFacility`): both call the one new rule `R.keepStoryBeasts` (`creature-rules.js`; one line each in `rules.js`). A waiting story beast cannot be released (`R.releaseBlocker`, and the Release button is hidden), because release says "the DM grants a new one", which is untrue for her.
   The conversion report (`strip-back-rules.js`) now says plainly: the House order for Elara's wyvern carries on, but the Pen already holds a wyvern, so it will wait for a home when it completes (it is added to the list of waiting beasts then; it is not lost); and **a new line** names every beast already waiting, with no order, because the Pen already holds one of its kind ("Skyrender is waiting for a home, because the Beast Pen already holds a wyvern. It stays on the waiting list; nothing is sent away." / "These beasts are waiting ... Skyrender (a wyvern) and Swiftwing (a griffin)."). The conversion itself still creates and removes no creature (audit check I5 unchanged), is still idempotent, and the audit is clean on a new v3 fixture (a housed wyvern, a waiting wyvern, a waiting griffin and Elara's order).
2. **The Pen's panel keeps its place.** `keepView` (tool.js, wrapped round the facility panel's redraw) keeps how far every scrolled box was scrolled, the focus on the same control by its `data-test` (or the one that replaces it: "Return X" for "Take X out", a beast's Return/Take/Rename button when Confirm bond goes), and every open `<details>` (the Chronicle). It applies to every facility panel, not only the Pen's. Tested in `tests/e2e/beast-pen-review.test.js` (scroll 977 became 181 before the fix; the check fails on the old code).
3. **Missing coverage added** (`tests/rules/beast-pen.test.js`, a group "the independent review's fixes"): handler history after a save and reload (who, which bond, why, in order); the first handler; a waiting milestone cleared by a handler change; the over-limit beast (two griffins) acclimatised, taken out and earning days, and surviving a reload; one level for each scene across a 35-day and a 60-day wait (Bond 2, then the prompt is Bond 3, then Bond 4 because 35 is over 30; Bond 5 takes a fourth confirmation); exactly one Chronicle line per confirmation and none for a refusal; temperament trimming and clipping; the Regional Variety; an empty note refused; take out with no handler refused; `R.nextStepLine` in every state up to Bond 5; and a reload while a day is half done (a beast away, stage "orders") counting that day once, with the tool's resume loop mirrored.
4. **Fresh Explorer day.** `penCtxNow()` reads `tsi.explorer.save` again at the moment of the click, right before `R.acclimatiseBlocker`, `R.acclimatise` (again after the dialog, which may stay open for minutes), `R.takeOut` and `R.returnToPit`. A click a moment after Make Camp is refused with "The Bastion is catching up with the Explorer's day". The e2e test freezes the Bastion's own 2-second look (its first read never returns) so the race is deterministic.
5. **A failed roll's 3-day wait stays across a handler change** (`R.assignHandler` no longer deletes `d.acclimatise`; the Bonding Days still start again). Tested for the lock, the count of tries, and Days 21, 22, 23.
6. **A damaged entry in `creatures` no longer stops the conversion.** The two loops (`beasts.forEach` and the "learned" filter) now run over `beasts = creatures.filter(isObj)`. A fuzz test tries several hundred shapes (null, text, numbers, empty objects, wrong shapes) in `creatures`, `pendingOrders`, `builtExtras`, `builtPlots`, `facilityLevels`, `repairs`, `research` and in each creature's and bond record's fields: the conversion finishes, the audit is clean, the result loads, and converting again changes nothing. **A second finding from the fuzz:** the Pen step also deleted `research.<old id>` and the audit insists research comes back exactly as it was, so a stray research entry failed the whole conversion. None of the three old buildings ever had a research tree, so the step no longer touches `research` (the Bastion drops a tree it does not know when it loads).
7. **Clear extra builds** refuses (with `R.clearBuildsBlocker`, asked again after the question in case a beast was housed meanwhile) while beasts live in a standing or half-built Pen, in plain English, and clears nothing.
8. **"Other beasts" stall** for creatures whose kind has no stall (a save from a newer copy): name, handler, Rename, Release for one that waits, Chronicle. `R.speciesHome` now falls back to the Pen so a line never says "at the its home".
9. **`$` in a name**: the House order's label is rewritten with a function replacement. The rest of the creature, Pen and conversion code was searched for the same pattern (a string used as a `replace` replacement): this was the only one.
10. **Acclimatise row**: the cost line follows the selected beast's kind (`R.acclimatiseDcText`: one kind's DC, the shared DC, or a range "DC 20 to 23"), and the note under the row is built from the data (`R.acclimatiseNote`: skill, DC or range, the days to wait). The developer sentence and the fixed "20" and "3 days" are gone from `facilities-data.js` (its own note is now plain and number-free). `guide.html` still tells Harry where the DC lives; that is a how-to for him, not what the DM sees in the Bastion.
11. **Wording and forecast.** (a) The one-line summary says "The three beast buildings became one Beast Pen" (or "two", "The beast building became the Beast Pen") and "A Beast Pen was built for your beasts"; "N places removed" now counts only places that really went. (b) The free-Pen sentence is singular for one beast ("Your beast had no building left standing ... It is still there.") and plural otherwise; tested with 1 and 3. (c) `campaign-rules.js`: a save below version 4 read before the Bastion opens forecasts **one** "The Beast Pen is built." (the one ready first; none if one is already built or a Pen is already in a slot), a converted (version 4) save is read as it is. Left alone on purpose: an order's saved label ("Griffin Tower: House a Griffin (Swiftwing) is complete.") is shown as saved until the Bastion converts it (the existing campaign test expects it).
12. **CSS.** The five bond bars have their own class (`tsi-bas-bond`, no pointer cursor, no hover brightening, no gold-button padding); `tsi-bas-pill` stays for the real gold pill buttons. A long word or link wraps inside its stall (`overflow-wrap: anywhere` and `minmax(0, 1fr)` grids). The e2e test puts a 40-letter name, a 34-letter word and a 118-character link in a beast's text at 1707 x 930: the stall overflowed by 44px before, by 0 after.
13. **Beast Handler Bond page.** At 1707 x 930 the sticky tab bar covered the five bond names and the "Ready" line. The picture on a laptop is now `clamp(220px, 100vh - 650px, min(44vh, 430px))`: 280px at 1707 x 930, the full 430px at the TV's 1920 x 1080. A new check in `beasthandler-bond.test.js` looks at what is under the middle of each bond name, the Bonding Days line, the kind line and the Ready line at both sizes (and at 1707 x 1067), away and ready; it fails on the old CSS.

## Choices I made myself

- Story beasts are never released, and their order's cancel or the Pen's demolition records them waiting (not asked for, but the same loss). Reversible by editing `R.releaseBlocker`.
- A waiting Elara's wyvern whose kind stays full waits for as long as the Pen holds a wyvern: the Pen never sends a housed beast away, so she can be housed only if the Pen's one-of-each limit is raised in `creatures-data.js` (`pen.perKind`). Nothing is lost.
- The page scroll for the Beast Handler is not zero (the connection line under the tab bar sits about 25 to 60px below the window on a laptop); everything above the bar, the bar itself and the bond card are fully in view.
- No order label is rewritten in the pre-conversion forecast.
