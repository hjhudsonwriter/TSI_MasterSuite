Other Buckbear species (no images other than Broadcrown as not yet needed):
1. Broadcrown
 * Region: the old-growth forests around Nightwood, Northern Province (Clan Blackstone).
 * Appearance:
   * the largest variety;
   * a shaggy, dark-brown coat with tawny-gold tips, heaviest in a ruff around the neck and shoulders;
   * tall, wide pale antlers with many upturned points, forming a crown;
   * amber eyes and long, pale claws;
   * usually found among moss, ferns and forest streams.
 * Lore link: the Heartwood Ritual's Rootbound Buckbear could be read as a Broadcrown corrupted by the Veinwood. Only adopt that if you want it.
2. Shovelhorn
 * Region: the frozen country around Coldpass, North Isle (Clan Karr).
 * Appearance:
   * stocky and heavily built;
   * a thick double coat from cream to smoke-grey;
   * small, rounded ears;
   * broad, flat antlers like a moose's, which it uses to scrape snow off food.
3. Cragbuck
 * Region: the slopes of The Fangs, Midland Province (Clan Bacca).
 * Appearance:
   * lean and long-legged;
   * a slate-grey coat with silver flecks;
   * short antlers with few points, swept back close to the skull so they don't catch on rock;
   * a sure-footed climber.
4. Fenwallow
 * Region: the marshes and waterways of the Eastern Province (Clan Slade).
 * Appearance:
   * low and heavy;
   * an oily, dark-brown coat that sheds water and is usually caked in mud;
   * wide, splayed paws for bog ground;
   * short, forward-curving antlers often hung with reeds;
   * a strong swimmer.
5. Saltcoat
 * Region: the coast and forest edges of the East Isle (Clan Rowthorn).
 * Appearance:
   * the smallest variety;
   * a russet-red coat bleached paler at the tips by salt and sun;
   * narrow antlers with a slight forward hook;
   * forages rock pools and the tideline for shellfish and fish.

Fellcat Regional Varieties (no images other than Lichenback as not yet needed):
1. Mistveil (your base description)
 * Region: the coast and fog banks of Bleaker's Bay, North Isle (Clan Karr).
 * Appearance:
   * a thick silver coat with faint smoke-grey rosettes;
   * tufted ears and oversized paws;
   * pale grey-green eyes;
   * fur that holds a damp sheen from the sea air.
 * Camouflage: sea mist.
2. Whiteout
 * Region: Icepoint.
 * Appearance:
   * the palest and heaviest variety;
   * an almost pure white coat with barely visible markings;
   * the deepest fur, with fur-covered paw pads;
   * a long, thick tail it wraps over its face when resting;
   * ice-blue eyes.
 * Camouflage: snow.
3. Lichenback
 * Region: the northern highlands.
 * Appearance:
   * leaner, built for rock rather than snow;
   * a grey coat mottled grey-green like lichen, turning frost-white in winter;
   * the longest ear tufts and a shorter tail;
   * hard, scarred paw pads.
 * Camouflage: frost-covered stone.
4. Tawnyfell
 * Region: the scree and lower slopes of The Fangs, Midland Province (Clan Bacca).
 * Appearance:
   * a shorter coat, smoke-brown to tawny-grey;
   * longer legs;
   * hunts at dusk below the snowline.
 * Camouflage: scree and twilight.
5. Smokecoat
 * Region: the high gorges of the Shadow Mountains, Western Province (Clan Farmer).
 * Appearance:
   * the smallest and rarest variety;
   * a charcoal-grey coat with near-black rosettes;
   * hunts at night.
 * Camouflage: darkness and shadowed rock.
