Pelagosi Puzzle Trials: sounds still to come

The Tidal Sequence ("The Absent Tide") has places for these .mp3 sounds. None
exist yet, so those moments are silent. Put each file in this folder with
exactly this name, then ask a Claude Code session to switch it on (ready: true
in tools/pelagosi/data/pelagosi-data.js). The five .wav files already here
stay as they are.

  chamber-bed.mp3         quiet ambient bed, loops (about 12 seconds), played softly
  water-stir.mp3          pressure rises to Stirring
  pressure-rise.mp3       pressure rises to Rising
  current-reverse.mp3     pressure rises to Reversing (pressure-rise.mp3 played backwards, made offline)
  tidal-surge.mp3         the chamber surges
  basin-wake.mp3          the outer ring is solved and the basin wakes
  anchor-release.mp3      the Anchor is taken out (Current confirmed)
  knock-1.mp3             the first of the three knocks
  knock-2.mp3             the second knock
  knock-3.mp3             the third knock, the heaviest
  water-backward.mp3      water running backward at the end (water-flow.mp3 played backwards, made offline)
  absence-void.mp3        optional: the hush as the Absence begins
  inscription-reveal.mp3  optional: a wall line becomes whole
  tide-return-toll.mp3    optional: the tide timer raises the pressure

Also keep water-flow.mp3 here as the source for water-backward.mp3. It is not
played by the tool, so it has no entry in the data file.
