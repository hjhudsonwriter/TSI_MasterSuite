Pelagosi Puzzle Trials: five sounds still to come

The Tidal Sequence has a place for five more sounds. The old tool named them,
but the files were never made, so those moments are silent for now:

  water-stir.wav       pressure rises to Stirring
  pressure-rise.wav    pressure rises to Rising
  current-reverse.wav  pressure rises to Reversing
  tidal-surge.wav      the chamber surges
  basin-wake.wav       the central basin wakes

When you have them, give them to a Claude Code session and ask it to add the
Pelagosi sounds. It will put them in this folder with exactly these names and
switch each one on in tools/pelagosi/data/pelagosi-data.js (ready: true).
