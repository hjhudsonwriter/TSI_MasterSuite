The Explorer's soundscape (guide 5, added 5 November 2026): background sound that follows the table, played from the Player View (the TV window).

Save the files in this folder with these names. Each one is a loop of 1 to 3 minutes (or shorter, if it loops well), free to use, roughly even in loudness.
The campfire, rain and storm beds reuse tools/explorer/assets/sfx/campfire.mp3, rain.mp3 and storm.mp3 by their path, so they are not here.
To use a different file name or kind (mp3, wav and ogg all work), change it in tools/explorer/data/soundscape-data.js. A file that is not here is skipped
quietly, and the Sound control (the Soundscape button in the top bar) says "file missing".

  coast.wav       surf on rocks, gulls, wind                         plays on The North Isle and The East Isle
  forest.mp3      wind in pines, distant birds                       Northern Province (East and West)
  marsh.mp3       reeds, frogs, still water, insects                 Eastern Province (North and South)
  plains.mp3      open wind over grass and fields                    Southern Province (East and West), Western Province (South)
  mountain.mp3    high thin wind, distant echoing bells or stone     Midland Province
  town.mp3        a market crowd at a low murmur, a far-off bell     while a town's card is on the TV
  tavern.mp3      an inn interior: chatter, mugs, a fire             a mood (Mood menu, or {sound: tavern})
  night.mp3       crickets, an owl, a light breeze                   after Make Camp, until the party moves again
  blizzard.mp3    howling wind with driven snow                      White Blizzard weather, over the road sound
  heat.mp3        a dry heat drone: cicadas, a still hot wind        Sun & Heatwave weather, over the road sound
  combat.mp3      tense battle music, loopable, no vocals            the combat mood while a fight is with the Combat Tracker
  mystery.ogg     a low, uneasy drone                                a mood (Mood menu, or {sound: mystery})

Levels were measured and set in data/soundscape-data.js so they sound about the same; the files themselves are as supplied.
