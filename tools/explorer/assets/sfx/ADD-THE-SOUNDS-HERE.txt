The Explorer's sound effects (added 30 October 2026).

Save the sound files in this folder with these names (mp3 is best; to use a different
name or kind of file, change it in tools/explorer/data/sfx-data.js):

  footsteps.mp3   grassy footsteps. Plays only while the heroes are moving. Should loop cleanly.
  campfire.mp3    a crackling campfire. Plays from Make Camp through the fade, and on while a
                  campfire event is open. Should loop cleanly.
  rain.mp3        rain. Loops while rain weather is active.
  storm.mp3       thunder and rain. Loops while storm weather is active.
  wing-flap.mp3   a wing flap. Plays once, together with raven.mp3, when "The Ironbow sends word..." opens.
  raven.mp3       a raven's caw. Plays once, at the same moment as wing-flap.mp3.

A file that is not here is skipped quietly (the Sound panel in the Explorer says "file missing").
